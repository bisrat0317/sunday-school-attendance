1. System Overview & Core Capabilities

The Bete Yared Sunday School Attendance & Student Management System is a cloud-ready platform tailored to automate attendance taking, maintain clean student directories, streamline family/sibling pastoral care, and prevent dropouts through real-time attendance alerts.

Role Integrity & Scoped Access

Two distinct user levels ensure maximum data security. Administrators oversee scheduling, student records, and reports, while Encoders have access limited strictly to the sessions assigned to them.

Draft vs. Finalized Locking

Encoders can save in-progress sessions as Drafts. Once Finalized, the session is securely locked against further edits to preserve audit compliance.

Ethiopian Dual-Time Support

Built-in Ethiopian time conversion bridges international standard time and traditional parish hours (e.g., 09:00 - 11:00 EAT automatically renders as ከጠዋቱ 3:00 - 5:00 ሰዓት).

Cross-Category Makeup Classes

Students attending make-up sessions in another grade are recorded with a Cross-Category badge, disabling duplicate marking in their home class and crediting their attendance.

3. Detailed Module Specifications

3.1 Student Management & Family Sibling Grouping

The student registry captures 10 distinct attributes per student: First Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name, Christian (Baptismal) Name, Age, Primary Phone, Emergency / Guardian Contact, Profession/School, Previous Church Service, and Category.

Automated Sibling Grouping Algorithm

The system executes an automated match across father_name and mother_name. Siblings attending different departments (e.g. one in Child, one in Grade 5, and one in Youth) are organized under a single household banner in the Families directory, allowing holistic pastoral coordination.

3.2 Session Scheduling & Recurrence Engine

Flexible Recurrence Series

Schedule once, or generate weekly/monthly batches (4, 8, 12, 16, or 24 weeks). The system verifies that no schedule overlap occurs in the same category.

1-Click Continue / Copy Session

Easily duplicate a prior session to next week (+7 days) or next month (+1 month) with all encoders and topics pre-filled.

3.3 Attendance Recording & Locking Rules

Present (ተገኝቷል)

Student physically attended the session.

Absent (ቀረ)

Student missed class without excuse.

Permission (ፈቃድ)

Official leave/excuse with remarks.

Data Integrity Safeguards

  • Future Date Block: Attendance cannot be marked prior to the actual session date.
  • Finalized Lock: Finalized attendance is locked for encoders. Only Administrators can adjust locked records.
  • Upcoming-Only Session Edit: Session details and assigned encoders can only be modified for upcoming dates (session_date ≥ today).

3.4 Cross-Category Attendance Workflow

When a student takes a make-up class in another category:

1
Add to Active Sheet: The encoder clicks "+ Add Student from Other Category", searches the student's name, and adds them.
2
Cross-Category Badge: The student is marked Present with an amber "Cross-Category" label.
3
Home Category Disabled State: In their home category sheet on that same date, their entry is disabled and tagged "Attended in other class", preventing duplicate marking.

3.5 3-Consecutive Absent Urgent Alerts

A real-time sliding window scans historical records per category. When a student misses 3 consecutive sessions in their home category without an excused cross-attendance:

Immediate Action Triggers:

The 3-Absent Alerts tab displays their profile, last present date, total absences, and provides two 1-click dial buttons: Call Student and Call Emergency Contact for instant pastoral outreach.

4. Step-by-Step User Manual

A. User Guide for Attendance Encoders (መዝጋቢዎች)

1
Login to Portal: Enter your encoder username and password.
2
Find Assigned Session: Navigate to the Sessions (የትምህርት ክፍለ-ጊዜያት) tab. Sessions assigned to you will feature a green "Assigned to You" badge.
3
Open Attendance Form: Click Take Attendance (መገኘት መዝግብ).
4
Mark Attendance: Use Mark All Present for quick bulk marking, or select individual status pills for each student. Add notes for permissions.
5
Save Draft vs. Finalize:
• Click Save Draft if class is still ongoing and you need to finish later.
• Click Finalize & Save once complete to lock the attendance record.

B. User Guide for System Administrators (አስተዳዳሪዎች)

1
Registering New Students:
Go to Students → click "+ Register New Student" → Fill in student and parental details → Click Save.
2
Scheduling a Class & Assigning Encoders:
Go to Sessions → click "+ Create New Session" → Fill title, date, start/end time (Ethiopian time preview updates automatically) → Check one or more encoders in the Assigned Encoders checklist → Choose optional recurrence (e.g., Weekly 12 weeks) → Click Create.
3
Editing Upcoming Sessions & Re-assigning Encoders:
Click the Edit () icon on any upcoming session → Adjust title, time, or check/uncheck encoders → Click Save Changes.
4
Managing 3-Absent Alerts:
Open the 3-Absent Alerts tab → Review flagged students → Click Call Student or Call Emergency for immediate telephone outreach.
5
Exporting Category Matrix Reports:
Open Category Matrix & Export → Select Category filter → Click Export to Excel for parish reporting.
6
Bulk Importing Students from Excel:
Go to Category Matrix & Export → click "Import from Excel/CSV" → Download the template → Fill in your data → Upload & confirm the pre-validation table.
7
User Management:
Go to User Management to create new Encoder accounts or reset forgotten passwords.
