1. System Overview & Core Capabilities

The Bete Yared Sunday School Management System is a cloud-ready platform designed to automate attendance recording, manage comprehensive student and family profiles, power an academic grading and assessment engine, generate individual single-page student report cards, and provide proactive pastoral follow-up.

Scoped Access & Operational Integrity

Clean role segregation between Administrators (handling scheduling, student profiles, assessments, report cards, and promotions) and Attendance Encoders (restricted strictly to assigned class rosters).

Draft vs. Finalized Locking

Encoders can save ongoing sessions as working Drafts. Once Finalized, attendance sheets are locked against unauthorized alterations to ensure strict historical compliance.

Ethiopian Dual-Time Engine

Bridges international 24-hour schedules with traditional Ethiopian parish hours (e.g. 09:00 - 11:00 EAT renders automatically as ከጠዋቱ 3:00 - 5:00 ሰዓት).

Grade Management & Report Cards

Assessment weighting, batch mark entry sheets, configurable pass/fail thresholds, class rankings, and 1-click single-page printable report cards with official church logo branding.

3. Detailed Module Specifications

3.1 Student Management & Family Sibling Grouping

The student registry captures 10 distinct attributes: First Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name, Christian (Baptismal) Name, Age, Primary Phone, Emergency / Guardian Phone, Profession/School, Previous Church Service, and Category.

Automated Sibling Grouping Algorithm

The system executes an automated match across father_name and mother_name. Siblings attending different departments (e.g. one in Child, one in Grade 5, and one in Youth) are automatically organized under a single household banner in the Families directory, allowing unified pastoral care and parent notifications.

3.2 Session Scheduling & Recurrence Engine

Flexible Recurrence Series

Schedule one-time classes or generate weekly/monthly batches (4, 8, 12, 16, or 24 weeks). The system verifies that no schedule collision occurs in the same category.

1-Click Continue / Copy Session

Easily duplicate a prior session to next week (+7 days) or next month (+1 month) with all encoders and course information pre-filled.

3.3 Attendance Recording & Locking Rules

Present (ተገኝቷል)

Student physically attended class.

Absent (ቀረ)

Student missed class without excuse.

Permission (ፈቃድ)

Official leave/excuse with reason.

Attendance Data Safeguards

  • Future Date Guard: Attendance cannot be marked prior to the actual session date.
  • Finalized Lock: Finalized attendance is locked for encoders. Only Administrators can adjust locked records.
  • Upcoming-Only Session Edit: Session schedule and assigned encoders can only be modified for upcoming dates (session_date ≥ today).

3.4 Cross-Category Attendance Workflow (Make-up Classes)

When a student takes a make-up class in another category:

1
Add to Active Sheet: The encoder clicks "+ Add Student from Other Category", searches the student's name, and adds them.
2
Cross-Category Badge: The student is marked Present with an amber "Cross-Category" label and receives attendance credit.
3
Home Category Disabled State: In their home category sheet on that same date, their entry is disabled and tagged "Attended in other class", preventing duplicate marking.

3.5 3-Consecutive Absent Urgent Alerts & Direct Dial

Proactive Pastoral Follow-Up

A sliding window automatically detects any student who missed 3 consecutive sessions in their home class. The 3-Absent Alerts tab provides instant 1-click buttons: Call Student and Call Emergency Contact for immediate telephone outreach and pastoral note recording.

3.6 Inactive Students & Academic Promotions

Inactive Student Registry

Maintains records of withdrawn or paused students with full past history preserved, offering 1-click reactivation at any time.

Annual Academic Promotion

Facilitates year-end transitions by advancing students to the next grade level with complete audit logging per student.

3.7 Grade Management, Gradebook Matrix & Report Cards

A. Assessment Creation & Weighting Engine

Administrators create and manage academic assessments per category. Key configuration parameters include:

Category & Semester

Assigned to specific Sunday school grades and terms (1st, 2nd, or Summer term).

Academic Year & Date

Full Ethiopian year support from 2026 down to 2018 ዓ.ም. (default 2025 ዓ.ም.) with dual date conversion.

Weight (%) & Max Score

Can weight 100% individually or split into multiple tests (e.g. 20%, 30%, 50%) totaling 100%.

B. Batch Student Mark Entry Sheet (ሞላ/ግቤት)

Rapid Matrix Mark Entry

Clicking "ውጤት መዝግብ / ሞላ" opens a spreadsheet-style modal populated with all students in the class. Teachers and administrators can enter scores rapidly using keyboard navigation (Tab/Enter), with live percentage previews, max-score enforcement, and 1-click batch saving.

C. Configurable Passing Threshold & Pure-Numerical Grading

Minimum Pass Threshold

Administrators configure the passing score percentage (default 50%) globally or per category.

Pure Numerical Ranking

No arbitrary letter grades (A, B, C, D). Students are evaluated on weighted total, overall percentage, class rank, and Pass (አልፏል) / Fail (አላለፈም) status.

D. Gradebook Summary Matrix & Excel Export

The Gradebook Matrix provides a master tabulation table of the entire class across all tests in the semester, displaying:

  • Individual scores and weighted points for each assessment held in the semester.
  • Cumulative Total Weighted Score (%) achieved out of 100%.
  • Class Rank (ደረጃ) calculated dynamically and ordered from 1st place downwards.
  • Final Result Badge: አልፏል (Pass) in green or አላለፈም (Fail) in red.
  • 1-Click Excel Export: Downloads the formatted gradebook spreadsheet for official records.

E. Batch Report Card Print System (Single-Page Layout)

Ink-Friendly Monochrome Single-Page Format

The Batch Report Cards (ውጤት ካርድ አትም) system generates individual student report cards formatted to fit exactly 1 student per page for clean printing.

Header & Identity: Church logo, Parish Title, Student Full Name, Baptismal Name, Category, Semester, Academic Year.
Academic Breakdown: Detailed table of all tests taken, max scores, scores obtained, weights, and weighted contribution.
Summary & Signatures: Total Score, Class Rank (ደረጃ), Status (አልፏል/አላለፈም), and official signature lines for Teacher and Admin.

4. Step-by-Step User Manual

4.1 Guide for Attendance Encoders (መዝጋቢዎች)

1
Login: Enter your encoder username and password on the login screen.
2
Locate Assigned Session: Navigate to the Sessions (ክፍለ-ጊዜያት) tab. Your assigned sessions feature a green "Assigned to You" badge.
3
Open Attendance Form: Click Take Attendance (መገኘት መዝግብ) on the session card.
4
Mark Attendance: Use Mark All Present for quick bulk recording, or toggle individual student badges between Present (ተገኝቷል), Absent (ቀረ), or Permission (ፈቃድ).
5
Add Make-up Students (Cross-Category): If a student from another category attends, click "+ Add Student from Other Category", search their name, and add them.
6
Save Draft vs. Finalize:
• Click Save Draft (ጊዜያዊ ረቂቅ) if the class is ongoing and you will complete it later.
• Click Finalize & Save (አጽድቀህ መዝግብ) once complete to lock the attendance record.

4.2 Guide for System Administrators (አስተዳዳሪዎች)

1
Student Registration & Import:
• Go to Students → click "+ Register New Student" → Fill in details → Click Save.
• To import in bulk, go to Category Attendance Matrix → click "Import from Excel/CSV".
2
Session Scheduling & Assigning Encoders:
Go to Sessions → click "+ Create New Session" → Fill title, date, time (check Ethiopian dual-time preview), select category, check assigned encoders, choose recurrence (e.g. Weekly 12 weeks) → Click Create.
3
Managing Pastoral 3-Absent Alerts:
Open the 3-Absent Alerts tab → Review flagged students → Click Call Student or Call Emergency for immediate telephone outreach.
4
Managing Assessments & Entering Marks:
• Open Gradebook (የውጤት መመዝገቢያ) → click "+ አዲስ ፈተና / ምዘና ፍጠር" → Set Category, Semester, Academic Year (`2025 ዓ.ም.`), Date, Weight, and Max Score.
• Click "ውጤት መዝግብ / ሞላ (Enter Marks)" on the assessment → enter student scores in the batch modal → click Save All Marks.
5
Gradebook Matrix & Report Card Batch Printing:
• Click የውጤት ሰንጠረዥ (Gradebook Matrix) to inspect class ranking and export to Excel.
• Click ውጤት ካርድ አትም (Batch Report Cards) → click አትም / በ-PDF አስቀምጥ to print all single-page report cards at once.
6
Academic Promotions & User Management:
• Go to Promotions (የክፍል ሽግግር) to advance students at year-end.
• Go to User Management (ተጠቃሚዎች) to create Encoder accounts and manage credentials.
